Delete specific data (without deleting your account)

You can remove data and keep your account. Do it in the app, or email us. This does not close your account — for that, see delete your entire account.

In the app:

  • Class blocks — Open My schedule, tap delete on any block.
  • Notes and note folders — Open the note or folder and delete it there.
  • Profile photo — Open Account and remove your photo.
  • Group to-dos and events — Open the to-do or event you created and delete it.
  • Quiz rounds and nudges — Delete them from the screen where you created them.
  • Group membership — Open group Settings, tap leave to remove yourself from a group.
  • Profile text — You can edit your display name, course or what you do, purpose, and timezone in the app.

If you can't find a delete control, or you want us to remove something we store (notes, class blocks, photo, profile info, push token, or other content listed above) without closing your account, email mrkrphl0705@gmail.com with subject sked data deletion request.

Delete your entire account

For permanent removal of your account and all data linked to you, delete it in the app under Account → Delete account (immediate), or use the account deletion page to email us (within 30 days).

Common questions

  • How do I join a group? — Ask a member for the 6-character group code, then enter it during onboarding or from group settings.
  • Why can't I sign in? — Google and Apple sign-in require the installed app (not Expo Go). Make sure you're on the latest build from the App Store or Play Store.
  • Who sees my schedule? — Only members of groups you've joined. Admins can set full detail or busy/free visibility per group.
  • How do I delete my account? — In the app: Account → Delete account (immediate). Or request account deletion by email (within 30 days). This removes your account and all data linked to you.
  • How do I delete some data but keep my account? — Delete specific data — notes, class blocks, photo, and other content in the app, or email us.
