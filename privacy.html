privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

sked ("we", "our", or "the app") helps student groups share class schedules and see when members are free. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Account info — When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your user ID and email address from that provider.
  • Profile info — Your display name defaults to the part of your email before the @ and you can edit it. Course or what you do, purpose, and timezone are optional.
  • Profile photo and files — Optional. If you add a photo, you pick it with the system photo picker. We store the photo and a cutout, plus any other files you upload, in InstantDB storage.
  • Pro — If you buy Pro on iOS, we store a Pro flag on your profile. Purchases go through Apple and RevenueCat.
  • Content you create — Personal class blocks (subject, room, day, times) and shared blocks in groups, groups and memberships, group to-dos (including assignments and reminders), group events with notes, subject folders and notes (and shares of them), group folders, quiz rounds, and nudges. Groups can have subject colors.
  • Last active time — Recorded automatically so we can understand usage and send occasional emails if you've stopped using the app.
  • Push token — If you allow notifications, we store an Expo push token so we can send alerts. Notification text may include to-do and event titles.
  • Analytics — We use PostHog to record app opens, sign-up, onboarding complete, and other core actions. PostHog uses an anonymous ID and may record your IP address and approximate city.
  • Crash and performance — We use Sentry for crash reporting and performance monitoring.

How we use data

  • Authenticate you and sync your content.
  • Show group members content you share in their groups.
  • Send push notifications you allow, and occasional re-engagement emails if you've stopped using the app. You can ask us to stop those emails.
  • Understand how the app is used, fix crashes, and improve reliability.
  • Process in-app purchases on iOS through Apple and RevenueCat.

Who can see your data

  • Group members — People in the same group can see content shared in that group.
  • Service providers — InstantDB (backend and storage), Expo (push), PostHog (analytics), Sentry (crash reporting and performance), Google and Apple (sign-in), Cloudflare (account deletion service), and Apple and RevenueCat (iOS in-app purchases). Each processes data under their own policies.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show ads and we do not collect an advertising ID.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Data retention & deletion

Data stays in your account while you use sked. You can delete notes, class blocks, your photo, and other content in the app without deleting your account — or email us. See delete specific data.

There are two ways to delete your account:

  • In the app — Account → Delete account. This is immediate.
  • By email — Write to mrkrphl0705@gmail.com. We handle email requests within 30 days. You can also use our account deletion page.

When you delete your account, this is what happens:

  • Sign-in account — Deleted last. Sign-in stops working.
  • Profile, push token, and Pro flag — Deleted.
  • Files under your account — Your profile photo and its cutout, and other uploads, are deleted.
  • Personal class blocks — Deleted. Shared blocks stay with the group.
  • Group memberships and your to-do assignments — Deleted.
  • Group to-dos you wrote — Kept if others are still in the group (other members' assignments and done marks stay on them). Your name is removed from them.
  • Reminders — Stay with their to-do. Deleted when that to-do is deleted, or when its empty group is deleted.
  • Nudges you sent, group events you wrote, your subject folders and notes (and shares of them), and quiz rounds you ran — Deleted.
  • Group folders you created — Handed to another member if the group remains. Deleted with an empty group.
  • Groups — You are removed. A group is deleted only when no other member remains. Subject colors stay with the group.

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel that in Apple settings. Pro ends when your profile is deleted.

Analytics and crash data held by PostHog and Sentry is not linked to your name or email and expires according to their retention policies.

Children

sked is intended for students (typically 13+). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do.
