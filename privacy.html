privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

sked ("we", "our", or "the app") helps student groups share class schedules and see when members are free. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Account info — When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and a user ID from that provider.
  • Profile info — Your display name defaults to the part of your email before the @ and you can edit it. Course (or what you do), purpose, and timezone are optional. You can add a profile photo; if you do, you pick it with the system photo picker and we store it in InstantDB storage.
  • Content you create — Class blocks (subject, room, day, times), groups and memberships, group to-dos, group events (including notes), notes and note folders, quiz rounds, and nudges.
  • Last active time — Recorded automatically when you use the app.
  • Push token — If you allow notifications, we store an Expo push token on your profile so we can send them.
  • Analytics — PostHog records app opens, sign-up, onboarding complete, and other core actions, using an anonymous ID. PostHog may also record your IP address and approximate city.
  • Crash and performance — Sentry collects crash reports and performance data so we can fix bugs and keep the app reliable.

How we use data

  • Authenticate you and sync your content across devices and groups.
  • Show group members content you share in those groups.
  • Understand usage, including from last active time.
  • Send occasional emails if you've stopped using the app (re-engagement). You can ask us to stop these emails.
  • Send push notifications you allow. Notification text can include to-do and event titles.
  • Improve reliability and fix bugs with Sentry, and understand product usage with PostHog.

Who can see your data

  • Group members — People in the same group can see content shared in that group.
  • Service providers — InstantDB (backend and storage), Expo (push), PostHog (analytics), Sentry (crash and performance), Google and Apple (sign-in), and Cloudflare (account deletion service). Each processes data under their own policies.
  • In-app purchases — Purchases on iOS go through Apple and RevenueCat.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show ads, and we do not collect an advertising ID.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Data retention & deletion

Data stays in your account while you use sked. You can delete individual class blocks or leave groups in the app without deleting your account. See delete specific data.

You can delete your entire account in two ways:

  • In the app under Account > Delete account — this is immediate.
  • By email to mrkrphl0705@gmail.com — handled within 30 days.

Deleting removes your account and all data linked to you: profile, photo, class blocks, notes and folders, quiz rounds, nudges, to-dos and events you created, group memberships, push token, and groups left empty. Groups that still have other members remain, but your content in them is removed.

Analytics and crash data held by PostHog and Sentry is anonymous and expires according to their retention policies.

See the account deletion page for steps.

Children

sked is intended for students (typically 13+). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do.
