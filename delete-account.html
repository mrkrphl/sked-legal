delete your account

Last updated: October 11, 2026

You can permanently delete your sked account and all data linked to you. There are two ways.

Delete in the app

Open Account, then tap Delete account. This removes your account immediately.

Request deletion by email

Email us from the address linked to your sked account (the one you used with Google or Apple sign-in). We handle email requests within 30 days.

request account deletion

Or email mrkrphl0705@gmail.com with subject line sked account deletion request.

What we delete

Deleting your account removes your account and all data linked to you:

  • Your profile (display name, course or what you do, purpose, timezone)
  • Your profile photo
  • Class blocks you created
  • Notes and note folders
  • Quiz rounds
  • Nudges
  • To-dos and events you created
  • Your group memberships
  • Push notification tokens stored on your profile
  • Groups left with no other members

Groups that still have other members remain, but your content in them is removed.

Analytics and crash data held by PostHog and Sentry is not linked to your name or email and expires according to their retention policies.

Before you go

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • You can leave individual groups in the app without deleting your account.
  • To delete only some data (notes, class blocks, photo, and so on) and keep your account, see delete specific data.
