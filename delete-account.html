delete your account

Last updated: October 11, 2026

You can permanently delete your sked account. There are two ways. This page says exactly what is deleted and what stays.

Delete in the app

Open Account, then tap Delete account. This removes your account immediately.

Request deletion by email

Email us from the address linked to your sked account (the one you used with Google or Apple sign-in). We handle email requests within 30 days.

What we delete

When you delete your account, this is what happens:

  • Sign-in account — Deleted last. Sign-in stops working.
  • Profile, push token, and Pro flag — Deleted.
  • Files under your account — Your profile photo and its cutout, and other uploads, are deleted.
  • Personal class blocks — Deleted. Shared blocks stay with the group.
  • Group memberships and your to-do assignments — Deleted.
  • Group to-dos you wrote — Kept if others are still in the group (other members' assignments and done marks stay on them). Your name is removed from them.
  • Reminders — Stay with their to-do. Deleted when that to-do is deleted, or when its empty group is deleted.
  • Nudges you sent, group events you wrote, your subject folders and notes (and shares of them), and quiz rounds you ran — Deleted.
  • Group folders you created — Handed to another member if the group remains. Deleted with an empty group.
  • Groups — You are removed. A group is deleted only when no other member remains. Subject colors stay with the group.

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel that in Apple settings. Pro ends when your profile is deleted.

Analytics and crash data held by PostHog and Sentry is not linked to your name or email and expires according to their retention policies.

Before you go

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • You can leave individual groups in the app without deleting your account.
  • To delete only some data (notes, class blocks, photo, and so on) and keep your account, see delete specific data.
