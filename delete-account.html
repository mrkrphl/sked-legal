delete your account

You can request permanent deletion of your sked account and associated data.

Request deletion

Email us from the address linked to your sked account (the one you used with Google or Apple sign-in):

request account deletion

Or email mrkrphl0705@gmail.com with subject line sked account deletion request.

What we delete

  • Your profile (display name, course, purpose)
  • All class blocks you created
  • Your group memberships (you will be removed from groups)
  • Push notification tokens stored on your profile

Groups you created may remain for other members. We remove you as a member and delete your personal data.

Timeline

We process deletion requests within 30 days. You'll receive a confirmation email when complete.

Before you go

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • You can leave individual groups in the app without deleting your account.
  • To delete only your schedule blocks, use the app — no account deletion needed.
